# Customer Support Playbook

## Channels and opening hours

Support answers email, chat and phone from Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00, and Saturday 9:00 to 14:00. Chat is the preferred channel because it lets agents share tracking links and screenshots.

## Response targets

First response targets are 4 business hours for email and 2 minutes for chat. Tickets tagged `urgent`, for example a missing high value order, must be picked up within 1 business hour.

## Identity check

Before discussing an order, agents confirm the customer's identity with the order number and the postal code of the delivery address. Agents never ask for passwords or full payment card numbers.

## Goodwill gestures

Agents can grant a goodwill voucher of up to 20 EUR without approval, for example after a late delivery. Anything above 20 EUR needs approval from a team lead.

## Escalation

If a customer threatens legal action or mentions a safety issue such as an overheating battery, the agent escalates to the team lead immediately and stops offering solutions in writing. Overheating devices must be reported to the product safety contact within 24 hours.
