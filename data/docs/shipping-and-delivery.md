# Shipping and Delivery

## Carriers and delivery times

Standard parcels are shipped with DHL and usually arrive within 1 to 3 working days in Germany. Large items such as monitors over 27 inches go by freight forwarder and take 3 to 5 working days. Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day leave the warehouse the same day.

## Shipping costs

Shipping is free for orders above 50 EUR. Below that the flat fee is 4.95 EUR. Express delivery costs 9.90 EUR and is only offered for parcels under 10 kg.

## Delivery to packing stations

Customers can choose a DHL Packstation as the delivery address. Freight items cannot be delivered to a Packstation.

## Lost or damaged parcels

If tracking has shown no update for 5 working days, support opens a carrier investigation. Customers who receive a damaged parcel should refuse it or report the damage within 7 days with photos. A replacement is sent once the carrier confirms the claim or after 10 working days, whichever comes first.

## Countries

We currently ship only to addresses in Germany. Orders with a foreign delivery address are cancelled automatically and get the status `cancelled`.
