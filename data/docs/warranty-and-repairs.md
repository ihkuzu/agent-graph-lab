# Warranty and Repairs

## Legal guarantee

Every product has the statutory guarantee of 2 years from delivery. During the first 12 months we assume a defect already existed at delivery unless we can show otherwise.

## Manufacturer warranty

Some manufacturers give an additional warranty. Laptops and smartphones usually come with a 2 year manufacturer warranty, smartwatches with 1 year. Customers can use either the manufacturer warranty or our guarantee; support should recommend whichever is faster.

## Repair process

Support creates a repair ticket and sends the customer a free shipping label. The repair partner reports a diagnosis within 5 working days. If a repair costs more than 60 percent of the product price, we offer a replacement or a refund instead.

## Out of warranty repairs

For devices outside the guarantee the customer receives a cost estimate first. The diagnosis fee is 29 EUR and is waived if the customer accepts the repair.

## Data on returned devices

Customers must remove passwords and personal data before sending a laptop, tablet or smartphone. The repair partner wipes devices that still contain data and does not create backups.
