# Returns Policy

Lindenhof Electronics GmbH sells consumer electronics online to customers in Germany. This page describes how returns work for customers and what support staff check before approving one.

## Return window

Customers can return any product within 30 days of delivery without giving a reason. The window starts on the delivery date recorded by the carrier, not on the order date. For orders placed between 15 November and 31 December the window is extended until 31 January of the following year.

## Condition of returned items

Items must be complete and include all accessories, cables and manuals. Opened packaging is accepted. Products with physical damage caused by the customer, such as cracked screens or liquid damage, are not accepted as normal returns and are handled as repair requests instead.

## Hygiene products

Earbuds and headphones can only be returned if the hygiene seal is intact. If the seal is broken the return is refused unless the product is faulty.

## Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 14 days after the warehouse receives the item. Shipping costs of the original order are refunded only if the whole order is returned. Return shipping is free for customers in Germany; the label is created in the customer account.

## Marking an order as returned

When the warehouse confirms receipt, support sets the order status to `returned` in the order system. Partial returns keep the status `delivered` and get a note in the order history.
