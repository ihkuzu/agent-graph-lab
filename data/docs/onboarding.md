# Onboarding

## First day

New employees start at 9:30 at the Nuremberg office. They receive their laptop, badge and accounts from IT and meet their buddy, an experienced colleague from the same team.

## First week

During the first week every new employee completes the data protection training and the security awareness training. Engineers set up their development environment following the README of the main repository and ship a small change to staging.

## Probation

The probation period is 6 months. Managers hold feedback talks after 1 month, 3 months and before the end of probation.

## Working time

The core hours are 10:00 to 15:00. Employees can work remotely up to 3 days per week after the first month.

## Equipment

Engineers can choose between a 14 inch and a 16 inch laptop. A second monitor, keyboard and mouse for the home office can be ordered once with a budget of 300 EUR.
