# IT Access and Accounts

## New accounts

IT creates accounts after HR confirms the contract in the HR system. New employees receive their laptop and login details on the first working day. Accounts for contractors expire automatically after 90 days unless the manager extends them.

## Passwords and multi factor authentication

Passwords need at least 14 characters. Multi factor authentication with the authenticator app is mandatory for email, VPN and the order system. Hardware security keys are required for administrators.

## VPN

The VPN is needed for the order system and the internal wiki when working outside the office. If the VPN client shows "certificate expired", restart the laptop while connected to the office network or contact the IT helpdesk; certificates renew every 180 days.

## Access requests

Access to production databases is requested through a ticket and must be approved by the team lead and the data protection officer. Read access to the analytics replica is enough for most reporting work and only needs team lead approval.

## Leaving the company

Accounts are disabled at 18:00 on the last working day. Laptops must be returned to IT within 5 working days.
