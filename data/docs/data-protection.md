# Data Protection

## Principles

We collect only the personal data needed to sell, deliver and support products. Personal data must not be copied to private devices or personal cloud storage.

## Retention periods

Order and invoice data are kept for 10 years because of German tax law. Support chat transcripts are deleted after 2 years. Server logs that contain IP addresses are kept for 14 days.

## Customer requests

Customers can ask for a copy of their data or for deletion. Such requests are forwarded to the data protection officer, who must answer within one month. Orders still inside the return window or tax retention period are not deleted but blocked from further processing.

## Analytics

Reports and dashboards use the analytics replica, where customer names are kept but email addresses and phone numbers are removed. Exports with personal data need approval from the data protection officer.

## Data breaches

A suspected data breach is reported to the data protection officer immediately. The authority must be notified within 72 hours if the breach is likely to put customers at risk.
